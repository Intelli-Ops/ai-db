1. Things to check before approving

Found by our own code, not by the AI. Each one is a way the report could be wrong even though the test months match.

  • Date filter: OrderDate is compared with '<= @end_date' (or BETWEEN). OrderDate holds a date AND a time, so rows with a time during the last day are left out. Safer: OrderDate < DATEADD(day, 1, @end_date).
Good to know about the joins (3)
  • Join Sales.SalesOrderHeader -> Sales.SalesTerritory - MAY DROP ROWS: Sales.SalesOrderHeader rows where TerritoryID is empty are left out.
  • Sales.SalesOrderDetail is joined straight to Production.Product (SalesOrderDetail.ProductID = Product.ProductID). The database does not declare this link. It matches on the primary key of Production.Product, so it cannot duplicate rows, but confirm it means what the SOP intends.
  • Join Production.Product -> Production.ProductSubcategory - MAY DROP ROWS: Production.Product rows where ProductSubcategoryID is empty are left out.

2. The written procedure and the SQL

Written procedure (SOP)
Monthly road bike sales in Europe

1. Take every sales order whose order date is within the report period
   (from the start date to the end date, both included).
2. Keep only orders from our European sales territories.
3. Look at each line of those orders and keep only the lines for road
   bikes.
4. For each territory, add up the quantity ordered and the line total.
5. Show three columns: Territory (the territory name), Units, Sales.
   Round Sales to 2 decimal places.
6. Sort by Sales, largest first.
SQL
-- Report:     Monthly road bike sales in Europe
-- Status:     PASSED - awaiting human review
-- Tested on:  {'start_date': '2025-03-01', 'end_date': '2025-03-31'} and held-out {'start_date': '2025-04-01', 'end_date': '2025-04-30'}
-- Written by: nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b on 2026-10-18, attempt 1 of 1
-- Parameters: @start_date date, @end_date date
-- Tables:     Production.Product, Production.ProductSubcategory, Sales.SalesOrderDetail, Sales.SalesOrderHeader, Sales.SalesTerritory, Sales.SpecialOfferProduct
WITH FilteredOrders AS (
SELECT
st.Name AS Territory,
sod.OrderQty,
sod.LineTotal
FROM Sales.SalesOrderDetail sod
JOIN Sales.SalesOrderHeader soh
    ON sod.SalesOrderID = soh.SalesOrderID
JOIN Sales.SalesTerritory st
    ON soh.TerritoryID = st.TerritoryID
JOIN Production.Product p
    ON sod.ProductID = p.ProductID
JOIN Production.ProductSubcategory psc
    ON p.ProductSubcategoryID = psc.ProductSubcategoryID
WHERE soh.OrderDate >= @start_date
AND soh.OrderDate <= @end_date
AND st.[Group] = 'Europe'
AND psc.Name = 'Road Bikes'
)
SELECT
Territory,
SUM(OrderQty) AS Units,
ROUND(SUM(LineTotal), 2) AS Sales
FROM FilteredOrders
GROUP BY Territory
ORDER BY Sales DESC;
