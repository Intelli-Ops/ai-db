2. The written procedure and the SQL

Written procedure (SOP)
Monthly sales by product category

1. Take every sales order whose order date is within the report period
   (from the start date to the end date, both included).
2. Look at each line of those orders and find the category of the
   product on that line.
3. For each product category, add up the quantity ordered and the
   line total.
4. Show three columns: Category (the category name), Units, Sales.
   Round Sales to 2 decimal places.
5. Sort by Category name, A to Z.
SQL
-- Report:     Monthly sales by product category
-- Status:     PASSED - awaiting human review
-- Tested on:  {'start_date': '2025-03-01', 'end_date': '2025-03-31'} and held-out {'start_date': '2025-04-01', 'end_date': '2025-04-30'}
-- Written by: nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b on 2026-10-16, attempt 1 of 2
-- Parameters: @start_date date, @end_date date
-- Tables:     Production.Product, Production.ProductCategory, Production.ProductSubcategory, Sales.SalesOrderDetail, Sales.SalesOrderHeader, Sales.SpecialOfferProduct
WITH FilteredOrders AS (
SELECT SalesOrderID
FROM Sales.SalesOrderHeader
WHERE OrderDate >= @start_date
AND OrderDate < DATEADD(day, 1, @end_date)
)
SELECT
pc.Name AS Category,
SUM(sod.OrderQty) AS Units,
ROUND(SUM(sod.LineTotal), 2) AS Sales
FROM Sales.SalesOrderDetail sod
JOIN FilteredOrders fo
    ON sod.SalesOrderID = fo.SalesOrderID
JOIN Production.Product p
    ON sod.ProductID = p.ProductID
JOIN Production.ProductSubcategory psc
    ON p.ProductSubcategoryID = psc.ProductSubcategoryID
JOIN Production.ProductCategory pc
    ON psc.ProductCategoryID = pc.ProductCategoryID
GROUP BY pc.Name
ORDER BY pc.Name;

The query first filters SalesOrderHeader to get SalesOrderIDs within the inclusive date range using a CTE. It then joins SalesOrderDetail to these orders, and traverses Product -> ProductSubcategory -> ProductCategory to get the category name. Aggregation sums OrderQty and LineTotal per category, with Sales rounded to 2 decimals. Results are ordered by Category name.

SELECT SalesOrderID
FROM Sales.SalesOrderHeader
WHERE OrderDate >= @start_date
AND OrderDate < DATEADD(day, 1, @end_date)

SUM(sod.OrderQty) AS Units,
ROUND(SUM(sod.LineTotal), 2) AS Sales

JOIN FilteredOrders fo
    ON sod.SalesOrderID = fo.SalesOrderID
