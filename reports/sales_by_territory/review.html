2. The written procedure and the SQL

Written procedure (SOP)
Monthly sales by territory

1. Take every sales order whose order date is within the report period
   (from the start date to the end date, both included).
2. For each sales territory, count the orders and add up the order
   subtotal (the amount before tax and freight).
3. Show three columns: Territory (the territory name), Orders, Sales.
   Round Sales to 2 decimal places.
4. Sort by Sales, largest first.
SQL
-- Report:     Monthly sales by territory
-- Status:     PASSED - awaiting human review
-- Tested on:  {'start_date': '2025-03-01', 'end_date': '2025-03-31'} and held-out {'start_date': '2025-04-01', 'end_date': '2025-04-30'}
-- Written by: gemini-3.5-flash-lite on 2026-10-06, attempt 1 of 5
-- Parameters: @start_date date, @end_date date
-- Tables:     Sales.SalesOrderHeader, Sales.SalesTerritory
SELECT T.Name AS Territory, COUNT(H.SalesOrderID) AS Orders, ROUND(SUM(H.SubTotal), 2) AS Sales
FROM Sales.SalesOrderHeader AS H
INNER JOIN Sales.SalesTerritory AS T
    ON H.TerritoryID = T.TerritoryID
WHERE H.OrderDate >= @start_date AND H.OrderDate < DATEADD(day, 1, @end_date)
GROUP BY T.Name
ORDER BY Sales DESC
